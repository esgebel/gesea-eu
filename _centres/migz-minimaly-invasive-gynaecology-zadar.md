MIGZ (Minimaly Invasive Gynaecology Zadar) is a center for continuous gynecological education organised as a first center in Croatia for GESEA certification and courses. The aim is to standardise and upgrade current MIGS training and to be region’s leader in state-of-the-art gynecological practice
