The Centre of Education and Training in Endoscopic Surgery (CETEC) was created by the Gynaecological & Obstetrics Department of CUF Porto Hospital together with Oporto’s University Medical School to provide more efficient and motivating training of endoscopic surgery for residents and specialists of Surgical Specialities. CETEC is an authorised centre by the European Academy of Gynaecological Surgery (EAGS) and the European Society of Gynaecological Endoscopy (ESGE) for education and training with the aim of European Certification in Gynaecological Endoscopy.
